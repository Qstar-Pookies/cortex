---
title: Daily Brief
tags: example
---

Summarise the last 24 hours of news about <TOPIC>.

Rules:
- Five bullets, twelve words max each.
- Every bullet carries a source domain in parentheses.
- If two sources disagree, say so in a sixth bullet.
- No adjectives that express an opinion. "Sharply" is an opinion.

If you cannot verify a claim, drop it rather than soften it.
